FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. Your first complete Deep Dive is free — just enter your email, verify with the 6-digit code we send, and you're in. No card, no trial period ticking down.

How does the OTP sign-in work?

Enter your email, we send a 6-digit one-time code. Enter the code and you're signed in — no password to remember, no OAuth, no data shared with third-party identity providers. Your session persists in your browser until you sign out.

What stocks can I analyse?

Any stock with a valid ticker — NYSE, NASDAQ, LSE (use .L suffix e.g. SHELL.L), FTSE 100, FTSE 250, DAX, CAC 40 and more. If yfinance carries it, Fintiq can analyse it. UK stocks and global markets are all supported.

Can I cancel my Deep Dive subscription?

Yes, any time. There's no minimum term. Email us at contactfintiq@gmail.com and we'll cancel immediately. If Stripe adds a customer portal, we'll link it here.

Is the Catalyst Atlas a subscription?

No. It's a one-off credit pack — you buy 10 company searches for £2.29 (or $2.99) and they never expire. Opening the same company again within 24 hours doesn't use another search. You can buy more packs whenever you need them.

Is this financial advice?

No. Fintiq Ltd provides information, quantitative analysis and educational tools to help you understand investment decisions — it is not regulated financial advice under FCA rules. The analyses are frameworks for structured thinking, not recommendations to buy or sell. Always do your own research and consider speaking to a qualified financial adviser before making significant investment decisions.

Do you store my card details?

No. Payments are processed by Stripe. Fintiq never sees or stores your card number — Stripe handles all payment data to PCI-DSS Level 1 standards. We only receive a confirmation that payment was made.

Can I use Fintiq for US and UK stocks?

Yes. Fintiq is built for global markets. US stocks work with their standard tickers (AAPL, TSLA, NVDA). UK stocks use the .L suffix (SHELL.L, BATS.L, AZN.L). Most liquid stocks worldwide are supported.
