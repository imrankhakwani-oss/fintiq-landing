How to read this page
Company's own move
How much the share price moved after taking out what the whole stock market did that day. If the market fell 1% and the share fell 3%, the company's own move was roughly −1% to −2%, depending on how strongly it usually follows the market.
Catalyst
A piece of news that moved the price: results, sales figures, a product launch, a lawsuit, a political story, and so on.
Day 0, 1 week, 1 month, 3 months
What the company's own move added up to on the news day, and then 5, 20 and 60 trading days later. This shows whether a reaction lasted or faded.
"Worked 6 of 8 times"
A simple count from this company's own history. Small counts are weak evidence, which is why cards with fewer than 4 examples are marked "Few examples".
Price range
Based on how much the price has been swinging (or, where available, what the options market is pricing in). It is a range, not a forecast of direction.
