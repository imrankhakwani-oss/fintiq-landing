2. Product Overview

Fintiq is built around two paid products — Stock Deep Dive (subscription) and Catalyst Atlas (credit pack) — supported by an ecosystem of free tools that drive discovery and repeat visits. The design philosophy is "Your Personal Stock Analyst": a retail investor should be able to do in 10 minutes what a sell-side analyst charges thousands for.

2.1 Core Product — Stock Deep Dive

Deep Dive is a seven-section AI-powered stock analysis tool. For any US or UK ticker the user enters, it generates:

  • Business Analysis — business model, revenue breakdown by segment/geography, moat assessment, risk summary
  • Valuation — DCF model (5-year, 3-scenario: bear/base/bull), intrinsic value range, Monte Carlo sensitivity on assumptions
  • Technical Analysis — RSI, MACD, 50/200-day moving averages, trend verdict, support/resistance levels
  • Risk & Position Sizing — Kelly criterion, volatility-adjusted position size, beta, max drawdown
  • Catalyst Tracker — summarises recent price catalysts and links directly to the Catalyst Atlas tool (see §2.3) for a full attribution breakdown
  • Investment Committee — 4-analyst AI panel (Value, Growth, Technical, Risk) debates the thesis; conviction score 0–100; Red Team plays devil's advocate
  • Trading Journal — save stocks to Watch/Open/Closed portfolio; R:R bidirectional calculator; connects to Portfolio tools

Each section is generated on demand using Claude AI (Anthropic), with Haiku for lightweight sections and Sonnet for the Committee and Red Team. Data is sourced from Yahoo Finance (yfinance), Financial Modeling Prep (FMP), and FRED.

2.3 Second Paid Product — Catalyst Atlas

Catalyst Atlas (fintiq.uk/catalyst-atlas.html) is Fintiq's second monetised product and operates on a separate credit-pack revenue model. It answers one question no other tool on the platform addresses: "Why did this stock move, and how does it normally respond to each type of news?"

The engine (catalyst_atlas.py v8) runs a rolling 250-day OLS regression that decomposes every daily price return into three systematic factors — market (SPY for US, ^FTSE for UK), sector (equal-weight sector ETFs, or Brent crude for energy companies), and interest rates (^TNX 10Y Treasury yield) — leaving an idiosyncratic residual that is attributable to company-specific events. It then:

  • Identifies every significant company-specific day over the past 3 years
  • Retrieves contemporaneous news via Tavily and uses Claude Haiku to label each event into 12 categories: Earnings & guidance, Sales & delivery data, Product & technology, Analyst rating, Deals & partnerships, Legal & regulation, Management & leadership, Politics & policy, Competition, Customer & industry demand, Market-wide news, Unexplained
  • Measures each catalyst's "footprint" — price behaviour from 5 days before to 60 days after — and scores how reliably each category moved the price and whether the move was sustained
  • Computes likely price ranges for 1 week, 1 month, and 3 months
  • Writes a plain-English Fintiq Analyst's View using Claude Sonnet — educational in tone, FCA-compliant (no buy/sell/hold language)

Results are cached 12 hours; news event labels are cached 30 days (Upstash Redis). UK shares are displayed in pounds. The tool is integrated directly into Deep Dive via an "Open Catalyst Atlas" button in the Catalyst Tracker section — users of the core product are funnelled into the credit-pack model automatically.

7. Regulatory Compliance

7.1 FCA Financial Promotions (s.21 FSMA 2000)

A full FCA compliance audit was conducted in November 2026. The platform was reviewed against the financial promotions rules under s.21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (FSMA). The key distinction applied throughout: data, analysis, and tools that inform research are permitted; personal recommendations ("you should buy X") are regulated activity requiring FCA authorisation.

All changes have been deployed as of November 2026:

  • Alpha Scanner: "Buy" signals renamed "Opportunity" throughout. Disclaimer updated to include "Signal labels are for research purposes only. Past performance is not indicative of future results."
  • Deep Dive: AI Challenge conviction language softened. Factor labels updated ("Avoid" → "No Alpha").
  • Pairs Trading: "BUY — underpriced" → "Long Signal — spread below mean"; "SELL — overpriced" → "Short Signal — spread above mean". Info bar updated to "not financial advice or a personal recommendation to trade."
  • Factor Screener: "These are the stocks worth researching further" → "These may warrant further independent research." Historical performance caveat added to all alpha figures.
  • Pension Planner: "real probability of never running out of money" → "estimated probability."
  • All tools: Footer disclaimer standardised to "For research purposes only. Not financial advice or a personal recommendation. Always conduct your own research and consider speaking to a qualified financial adviser before investing."

Acquirer / Licensee note: The platform is built for FCA compliance but the operating entity must hold FCA authorisation or appointed representative status for UK-facing deployment where any regulated activity is performed. The founder can provide a full compliance review document on request.

7.2 GDPR / Data Protection

User data stored: email address and subscription status only. No financial data, no trading history, no personal identifiers beyond email. Session tokens stored as SHA-256 hashes. OTP codes are single-use and expire after 10 minutes. Railway persistent volume stores auth.db in an EU-region data centre.

8. Security

A full security audit and hardening exercise was completed in November 2026. The following controls are in place:

8.1 Authentication & Sessions

  • Passwordless email OTP: 6-digit codes, cryptographically generated (Python secrets module), 10-minute TTL, single-use (deleted on successful verify)
  • Session tokens: 32-byte cryptographically random (secrets.token_urlsafe), stored as SHA-256 hashes in SQLite — raw token never persisted server-side
  • 30-day session TTL with server-side expiry check on every request
  • OTP brute-force protection: 5 failed attempts triggers 15-minute email lockout

8.2 Rate Limiting

  • OTP request endpoint: 5 requests per email per hour, 20 per IP per hour (Upstash Redis INCR/EXPIRE)
  • Fail-open: if Redis is unavailable, rate limiting degrades gracefully without blocking legitimate users

8.3 Payments

  • Stripe Checkout: server-generated checkout sessions only; no card data touches the Fintiq backend
  • Webhook signature: mandatory HMAC-SHA256 verification on all Stripe events; request rejected if STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET is not configured

8.4 API Security

  • CORS: restricted to fintiq.uk, www.fintiq.uk, app.fintiq.uk, localhost — no wildcard
  • All API credentials (Anthropic, FMP, Stripe, Brevo, FRED) in Railway environment variables — no hardcoded credentials in source code
  • SQL: all queries use parameterised statements (? placeholders) — no SQL injection risk
  • Admin endpoints: protected by REFRESH_TOKEN; debug endpoints removed from production code

8.5 Infrastructure

  • X-Robots-Tag: noindex on all API responses — prevents search engine indexation of api.fintiq.uk
  • Railway persistent volume: SQLite databases stored on /data — not in /tmp (which is wiped on cold starts)
