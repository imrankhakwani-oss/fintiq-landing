Inheritance Tax — A Key SIPP Advantage

Unlike your estate (property, ISAs, savings), a SIPP is currently outside your estate for Inheritance Tax purposes. This means your pension pot can be passed to beneficiaries free of the 40% IHT charge that applies to other assets above the nil-rate band.

SIPP (pension pot)
Outside estate — passes IHT-free to nominated beneficiaries. If you die before 75, drawn by beneficiaries tax-free. After 75, taxed as their income but no IHT.
ISA / Property / Savings
Inside estate — subject to 40% IHT above the nil-rate band (£325,000 + residence nil-rate band up to £175,000).
2027 rule change: The October 2024 Budget announced that SIPPs will be brought into estates for IHT from April 2027. Planning decisions made now should account for this change. Seek specialist IHT advice if this affects you.

Tax relief (relief at source): You pay the net amount into your SIPP. HMRC automatically adds 20% basic rate relief, grossing it up. If you pay 40% or 45% income tax, you can claim the additional relief via Self Assessment — this comes back to you as a cash refund, not into the pension pot.

Monte Carlo simulation: 1,000 independent paths are simulated. Each monthly step applies a log-normally distributed return drawn from the selected annual mean and volatility. Your gross monthly contribution is added each step. Results show the spread of outcomes — not a forecast.

Drawdown — PCLS vs UFPLS: PCLS (Pension Commencement Lump Sum) — you take up to 25% of your pot as a one-off tax-free sum (capped at £268,275), then draw down the remainder as taxable income. UFPLS (Uncrystallised Fund Pension Lump Sum) — each withdrawal is 25% tax-free and 75% taxable, spreading the tax-free entitlement across your lifetime.

Growth assumptions: Conservative 4% p.a. / 10% vol · Balanced 6.5% p.a. / 12% vol · Aggressive 9% p.a. / 16% vol. All figures are nominal. Inflation, provider charges, and investment costs are not modelled. The 4% sustainable withdrawal rate is a commonly used rule of thumb and is not guaranteed.
