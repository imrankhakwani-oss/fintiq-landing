The Efficient Frontier

Every point on the Efficient Frontier represents the highest possible expected return for a given level of risk (volatility). Portfolios below the frontier are suboptimal — you're accepting more risk than necessary for a given return. The goal is always to be on or as close as possible to the frontier.

⚖️

Sharpe Ratio & Capital Market Line

The Sharpe ratio = (Portfolio Return − Risk-Free Rate) / Portfolio Volatility. It measures how much return you earn per unit of risk. The "tangent portfolio" — where the Capital Market Line touches the Efficient Frontier — is the portfolio with the maximum Sharpe ratio. This is typically the optimal mix for most investors.

🔗

Diversification & Correlation

Holding assets that don't move in perfect lockstep reduces portfolio volatility without necessarily reducing expected returns. This is the only "free lunch" in finance. Low or negative correlation between your holdings is the key driver of the Efficient Frontier's curvature — the more diversified, the further left the frontier extends.
